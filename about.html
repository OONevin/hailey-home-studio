01 The story

Eleven years in design/build, now under one roof.

“HHS is a way for me to bring that experience, those relationships, and the resources I’ve built together under one roof.”

After 11 years in the design/build industry, I’m excited to introduce Hailey Home Studio.

Over the years, I’ve had the opportunity to work across many sides of the industry, build an incredible network of trade partners and vendors, and most importantly, work with clients I truly love and care about.

Hailey Home Studio is a concierge design, sourcing and procurement service. For designers, that means one point of contact instead of a sales rep at every vendor. For builders and homeowners, it means a simplified, seamless process from concept and specification through sourcing, ordering and installation.

I’m officially welcoming new design projects, trade partnerships and referrals, and I’m excited for what’s ahead.

Hailey Renee Schueneman

Sales & Design Consultant · Hailey Home Studio LLC
